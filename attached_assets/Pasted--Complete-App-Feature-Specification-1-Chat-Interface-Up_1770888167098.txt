That selected pet will appear sitting on top of the profile photo.

The pet will remain positioned on the profile photo unless changed.

🖼 Profile Background Animation:

A watermark-style animated background should appear.

The animation should cover 30% of the screen in a square format.

It should appear in full-screen style but limited to a 30% square area behind the profile section.
