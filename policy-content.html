Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2025  |  Last Updated: July 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how VAMPIRE ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use the VAMPIRE mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use VAMPIRE:

  • Account Information: Full name, username, email address, and password (encrypted)
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, bio, and any details you add to your profile
  • User Content: Videos, images, stories, posts, and comments you create or upload
  • Messages: Direct messages sent between users (stored securely)
  • Notifications: Likes, comments, follows, and activity on your posts
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent, and interactions within the App
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Authentication Data: OTP codes used for password reset (temporary, auto-deleted)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create, verify, and manage your user account
  • To display your content and profile to other users
  • To enable direct messaging and social interactions
  • To send OTP emails for password reset and account verification
  • To send push notifications about activity on your account
  • To personalize your content feed using AI recommendations (Google Gemini)
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam, and policy violations
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. Data Storage and Infrastructure

Your data is stored and processed using the following trusted third-party services:

  • DB Supabase & Neon: User accounts, posts, messages, comments, notifications, and all app database records
  • MEDIA Cloudinary: Videos, images, profile photos, and all media files uploaded by users
  • SERVER Render: Application server hosting and backend infrastructure
  • AI Google Gemini API: AI-powered features such as content translation and smart replies

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are stored using industry-standard bcrypt hashing and are never stored in plain text.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. Your information may be shared only in these limited cases:

  • With other users: Your public profile, posts, stories, and comments are visible to other users of the App
  • With service providers: Supabase, Neon, Cloudinary, Render, and Google (Gemini API) — only to operate the App
  • For legal compliance: If required by law, court order, or to protect user safety
  • Business transfer: In case of merger or acquisition, with prior notice to users

5. User-Generated Content

Content you upload (videos, images, posts, stories) may be visible to other users. You retain ownership of your content. By uploading, you grant VAMPIRE a non-exclusive license to display and distribute your content within the App. Content that violates our community guidelines may be removed.

Reported content is automatically hidden from the reporting user for 2 hours while under review.

6. Direct Messages

Direct messages between users are stored securely in our database. Messages support end-to-end encryption features. We do not read private messages unless required for safety investigations or legal compliance.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • OTP tokens are automatically deleted after use or after 10 minutes
  • Deleted posts and messages are permanently removed from our servers
  • You may request full account deletion at any time by contacting us

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain types of data processing
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time without affecting past processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at vampireofficial00@gmail.com

9. Children's Privacy

VAMPIRE is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has created an account, we will immediately delete their data. Parents or guardians who believe their child has registered should contact us immediately.

10. Security Measures

We implement the following security practices to protect your data:

  • All passwords are hashed using bcrypt encryption
  • All data transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Secure session management with server-side sessions
  • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure
  • Access to user data is strictly limited to authorized systems

11. Cookies and Local Storage

The App uses session cookies for authentication purposes only. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. Session data is cleared when you log out.

12. Third-Party Services

Our App integrates with third-party services. Each has their own privacy policy:

  • Google Gemini AI — ai.google.dev/terms
  • Cloudinary — cloudinary.com/privacy
  • Supabase — supabase.com/privacy
  • Render — render.com/privacy

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Post the updated policy in the App
  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top
  • Notify users via email or in-app notification for major changes

Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by applicable data protection laws. We are committed to complying with GDPR (for EU users), CCPA (for California users), and other applicable privacy regulations.
